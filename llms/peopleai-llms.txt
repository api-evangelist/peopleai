# People.ai - AI Revenue Platform for Sales Teams

> People.ai is an AI-powered revenue intelligence platform that helps enterprise sales teams increase productivity, boost pipeline visibility, and drive predictable growth. It automatically captures sales activity and contact data from email, calendar, and conversations, matches it into CRM (Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics 365), and surfaces forecasting, deal, and account insights. People.ai is a SaaS product with no public developer API; it integrates with the CRM and collaboration platforms it sits on top of.
